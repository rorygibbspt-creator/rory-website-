Same situation

Your teammate costs your team the win. What do you do next?

Your first feeling is normal. Your next response matters. Tap an athlete to see how it plays out.

  • Points the finger straight away.
  • Complains to others after the match.
  • Makes the teammate feel worse.
  • Brings tension into the group.

Likely outcome: short term release, less trust, weaker team culture.

  • Takes a moment to cool off.
  • Checks in with the teammate.
  • Talks about what the team can learn.
  • Keeps standards high without attacking the person.

Likely outcome: more trust, better learning, stronger team culture.
