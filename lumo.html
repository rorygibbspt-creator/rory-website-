Welcome to Planet Lumo

One planet. Many little worlds.

The Crystal Crew zip across Planet Lumo's districts on sporty adventures, with a fresh challenge waiting in every one. They have the talent but not yet the soft skills, and in each story that's exactly what they learn: how to put their talent to good use.

Every book ends with fun, interactive challenges: colouring, simple physical exercises and little tasks built around the value, so the story carries on off the page.
