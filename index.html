About me

A late start isn't a disadvantage.

I didn't pick up an oar until I was 18. Before that it was rugby and whatever else was going. Eight years later I was racing at the Tokyo Olympics, where we fell well short of our goal and very nearly crashed into another crew.

Three years of rebuilding later, my crew and I won gold in Paris. Now I've stepped away from international rowing, I spend my time backing the next generation of athletes, speaking, and writing children's books about sport, values and adventure.
