# Recording plan: one main talk, personal bits per school, no audio jump

The editable script lives in Claude Docs: https://claude.ai/artifact/WcGomhB7aFuuKpk5xtcedg
Once Rory has edited it, the animation in `docs/talks/parents/index.html` gets updated to match.

## The structure

| Piece | Recorded | Camera |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School intro, about 75 seconds | Per school | Full frame |
| Main talk, about 17 minutes | Once | Circle, bottom left |
| School line 1, about 10 seconds (Part 2, after "You get the rest") | Per school | Circle |
| School line 2, about 5 seconds (before the checklist) | Per school | Circle |
| School outro, about 45 seconds | Per school | Full frame |

About 3 minutes of recording per school. Renewal next year = the same 3 minutes again with the new year's details.

## Keeping the sound identical

Parents notice a jump in sound far more than a jump in picture. Five things stop it:

1. **Same spot, same mic, same settings, every time.** Record the main talk and every school's pieces in the home set,
   with the clip on mic in the same place on the same top. Tape marks on the floor for the chair and tripod.
2. **One voice clean up on everything.** Run every clip through the same voice enhancement in the editor
   (CapCut "Enhance voice", or Descript "Studio Sound") at the same strength. It evens out room sound and mic distance,
   so a clip recorded in March matches one recorded in September.
3. **A soft music bed under the whole video.** Very quiet, the same track from start to finish. It glues the joins together.
4. **Cut only at scene changes.** Every school line sits where the animation is already moving to a new scene,
   with a soft whoosh. The eye is on the movement, not the join.
5. **Match the energy.** Before recording a school's pieces, play back 20 seconds of the main talk on headphones,
   then record at the same pace and volume.

The full frame intro to circle switch is a visual change anyway ("let's get into it", then Rory shrinks into the circle),
so a small change in sound there reads as an edit, not a giveaway. An outdoor intro also works, because the picture explains
the different sound, but it means going outside for every school, so the home set is the default.

These videos are honest about being recorded. The aim is a polished film, not pretending it's live.

## From recording to finished video

1. Rory records the main talk scene by scene, reading from the teleprompter panel on the talk page.
2. Rory edits out retakes in CapCut or Descript, then exports **captions as an SRT file** (timed captions).
   That file is what lets every animation land on the right word. Send the SRT; the video itself is too big to send here.
3. The animation is re-timed to the SRT, so each pop, tick and highlight hits the moment Rory says it.
4. Background removal is done in CapCut or Descript ("Remove background"), no green screen needed.
5. The animation is exported as a video for each school (crest, colours, name swapped in).
6. Final assembly in CapCut: animation as the bottom layer, Rory's cut out camera in the circle, intro and outro
   full frame, school lines dropped in at their scene changes, music bed under everything.
