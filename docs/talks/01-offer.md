## What every school gets

1. **The main talk.** Rory's 20 minute talk, recorded once in studio quality and refreshed each year.
2. **A personal intro and outro, recorded fresh for that school.** About 60 to 90 seconds each.
   Rory speaks to the school by name and mentions things that are true for them: an event coming up,
   their values, their houses, a recent result, a word from the head. The school sends these details on a short form.
3. **Slides in the school's colours.** The talk's slides carry the school's crest, name and colours,
   in the same bold, simple style as Rory's social content.
4. **Ready to send.** An email the school can copy and paste to parents (or a note for staff before assembly),
   with the link and a line on why now.
5. **The extras for that talk.**
   - Back Them at Home: the one page parent checklist, *Seven ways to back your child at home*, as a printable PDF with the school's crest.
   - Built. Not Born.: a short set of discussion questions so a teacher can run five minutes of chat after it.
6. **Delivery.** A private, unlisted video link for the school to share, plus a download for playing in a hall without wifi.
   Turnaround: [10] school days from receiving the school's details.

## How booking works

1. The school picks a talk or the pack and pays (invoice, [payment terms to confirm]).
2. The school fills in a short form: school name, crest file, colours, who to thank, three to five details
   (events coming up, values, houses, a recent win), and the date they plan to send or show it.
