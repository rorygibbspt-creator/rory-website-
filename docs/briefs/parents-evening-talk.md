# Brief: the parents' evening talk

The first thing to build in the school talks workstream. Follow `CLAUDE.md` for brand, voice and writing rules.

## The format

- **20 minutes**, delivered to a parents' evening through a big screen. Rory is not in the room.
- Two ways to run it (build so both work):
  1. **Live on a video call**, shown on the school's screen, with an optional short Q&A after.
  2. **Pre-recorded**, so a school can play it any time. This is the passive income version: record once,
     license it to many schools, and refresh it each year.
- Audience: parents of children around 10 to 14 (prep school, and the year before senior school).
- Not just sport. The same ideas apply to music, exams, drama and anything a child works hard at.
  Rory's examples come from sport, but every point should land for a parent whose child plays the violin.
- Slides are a backdrop, not a script. Few words on screen, big and readable from the back of a hall.

## Running order (about 20 minutes)

| Time | Section | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Welcome | Who I am, why I'm on your screen tonight. |
| 1:00 | My story (about 6 min) | Rugby first, rowing at 18. Eight years later, Tokyo 2021, falling short. Three years rebuilding. Gold in Paris 2024. What made the difference was not talent. |
| 7:00 | The big idea | "Talent opens the door. Character walks you through it." Talent makes you fast; character makes you faster. And character is built at home, more than anywhere else. |
| 8:00 | Seven things that help at home (about 80 seconds each) | See below. |
| 17:00 | Your checklist | One slide to screenshot, with a QR code to download it. |
| 19:00 | Close | Thank you, and where to find more. |

### The seven things (keep each to one idea and one example)

1. **The car journey.** Talk after, not during. Let feelings settle first. Ask open questions ("What did you enjoy?") before any feedback.
2. **Tests and big moments.** Exams, matches, music grades and auditions feel the same to a child. Help with a simple routine, sleep and perspective.
3. **Fuel and sleep.** Keep it simple and general: regular meals, water, and most young people doing better on around 8 to 10 hours of sleep. Not medical or diet advice.
4. **Goals and targets.** Focus on the process they control, not just the result. Small steps, written down, checked together.
5. **Their future, their dreams.** Ask what they want, and listen. Schools, scholarships and ambitions should be their dream, supported by you.
6. **Support without pressure.** Praise effort and character, not just results. Your reaction is the mirror they look into.
7. **Challenge them.** High standards and kindness can go together. Let them struggle a little; that's where growth happens.

## The checklist (the thing parents keep)

- One page, screenshot friendly, in the brand style. Title along the lines of "Seven ways to back your child at home".
- One line per point above, written as an action ("After a match or exam, ask what they enjoyed before anything else.").
- QR code and short link to a page on the website where they can download it as a PDF.
- At the bottom, quietly: the Athlete Deck (93 cards, coming soon or on sale), the app waitlist (for them and their child),
  coaching enquiries, and the book. No hard sell.

## What to build

1. The slide deck (brand colours, Plus Jakarta Sans, Rory's illustration, the real card artwork where it helps).
2. Speaker notes or a full script for every slide, written the way Rory talks: plain, warm, honest, British English, no dashes.
3. The checklist as a printable one-page PDF.
4. A page on the website (for example `/checklist`) where parents download the checklist and can leave their email.
   It must match the existing site and use the shared header and footer (`src/` and `tools/build.py`).
5. For schools: a one-page information sheet (what the talk is, length, tech needed: screen, speakers, a laptop with
   a video call link or the video file) and a short outreach email.

## Rules

- Never invent stats, research findings or testimonials. Real testimonials only (see `CLAUDE.md`).
- Prices for the talk, coaching, app and courses are not confirmed. Leave them out or mark them as placeholders for Rory.
- Keep nutrition and wellbeing points general and sensible; no medical claims.
