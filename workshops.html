Team workshops · Thames Boat Club

Bring your team to the boathouse.

A team day built around how an Olympic gold medal crew works. A session in the rowing tank or out on the water, a keynote, and lunch together by the river.

Eight people. One rhythm.

Rowing is one of the most demanding team sports in the world. Eight people, one goal, and no room for ego. Your team spends a day inside that.

Most team building is forgettable. This isn't, because the boat is brutally honest. It only moves well when people genuinely work together. Afterwards we sit down and translate exactly what happened on the water into how your team works back at the office.

A day at Thames Boat Club

How a typical full day runs.

  1. Morning

    Arrival & welcome

    Coffee at the boathouse and a short framing of the day ahead.

  2. Session

    The tank or the water

    Hands on rowing in the indoor tank, or out on the river in a stable crew boat with full coaching. No experience needed.

  3. Talk

    The keynote

    A focused talk on teamwork, trust and standards, tied directly to what your team just felt in the boat.

  4. Lunch

    Eat together

    Proper food by the river. Where the real conversations happen and the lessons land.

What's included

Everything, sorted.

  • Coached session

    A tank or water session led by an Olympic champion, with full safety cover. Built for complete beginners.

  • Tailored keynote

    Written for your team's moment, whether that's a merger, a reset, or a group that's grown fast and lost its rhythm.

  • Lunch & takeaways

    Lunch together, plus a simple one page set of team principles to take back to work.
