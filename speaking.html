The talk

Performance. Relationships. Discipline.

I started rowing at 18, had the rowing equivalent of a car crash at my first Olympics in Tokyo, and spent three years rebuilding before winning gold in Paris.

Every talk is honest, specific to your audience and built on what I lived through in elite sport. No highlight reel, no stock slides.

Questions

Speaking, answered.

Do you mainly speak to schools or companies?
Both. Schools are my focus right now and I'm taking the keynote around the UK. For organisations I do keynotes, firesides and after dinner speaking. Tell me which you are and I'll tailor everything to suit.
What ages can you speak to?
From primary (the LUMO assembly, ages four to seven) right up to sixth form, plus adult corporate audiences. I adapt the language, length and stories to the room.
What kinds of events do you cover?
Assemblies, keynotes and workshops, sports days, speech days and prize givings, plus conferences, leadership offsites, after dinner and awards evenings.
Do you travel?
Yes. I'm based in London and travel across the UK as standard. International dates are very doable with a bit of notice.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the audience, format, location and date. Send your details and I'll come back with a clear answer.
