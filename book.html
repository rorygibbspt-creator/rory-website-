The Athlete: Built Not Born.

My first book. The mindset, habits and values I learned on the way to Olympic gold, written for young athletes aged 12 to 18 and the people who support them.

Buy on Amazon What it is (and isn't)
Honestly

What this book is, and what it isn't.

I'd rather you knew exactly what you're buying.

  • Short, practical lessons you can use straight away
  • Written from my own experience, from a late start at 18 to Paris 2024
  • For athletes of any sport, not just rowing
  • Easy for parents and coaches to read alongside their athlete
  • Not a sports science textbook
  • Not a training programme or a diet plan
What's inside

Lessons you can use

The things nobody teaches young athletes, but every good one learns.

Identity & success

Who you are beyond results, and confidence without arrogance.

Pressure & comparison

Nerves, mistakes in front of others and the social media treadmill.

Setbacks & selection

Not getting picked, injuries and feeling behind everyone else.

Consistency & energy

Why consistency beats motivation, plus food, sleep and avoiding burnout.

Coachability & team

Leading without being the loudest, and handling difficult coaches.

The long game

Why enjoyment matters, and what winning is really for.

Read it together.

It works best when a parent or coach reads it too. You end up with a shared language for the conversations that matter.
