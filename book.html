The book · Ages 12 to 18

The Athlete: Built Not Born.

The mindset, habits and values that build a champion. Written for ambitious young athletes and the people who support them, and drawn from my road to Paris 2024.

Buy on Amazon From £9.99
What's inside

Lessons you can use

Short, practical chapters on the things nobody teaches young athletes, but every good one learns.

Identity & success

Who you are beyond results, what success really looks like, and confidence without arrogance.

Pressure & comparison

Nerves, peer pressure, mistakes in front of others, and stepping off the social media treadmill.

Setbacks & selection

Not getting picked, injuries, setbacks and feeling behind everyone else.

Consistency & energy

Why consistency beats motivation, plus food, sleep and avoiding burnout.

Character & team

Coachability, leading without being the loudest, and handling difficult coaches.

The long game

Rebuilding confidence, why enjoyment matters, and what winning is really for.

Read it together.

The book works best when a parent or coach reads it too. It gives you a shared language for the conversations that matter.

Talent opens the door. Character walks you through it.
