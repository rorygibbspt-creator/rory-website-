Practical cards for young athletes, parents and coaches, in any sport. One idea per card. Pick one, talk about it, try it at training. Think better, train better, respond better.

Card 01: Start here.

Every card follows the same simple shape. One clear idea, a picture to remember it by, and something to do with it. Built to sit on the kitchen table or in a kit bag.

Who it's for

One deck. Three ways to use it.

Young athletes

Any sport, any level. Pull a card before training and pick one thing to work on that day.

Parents

A way into the conversations that are hard to start. Selection, nerves, a bad game or a big win.

Coaches

A ready made topic for a team talk or a one to one, with questions to get young athletes talking.
