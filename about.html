About me

A late start isn't a disadvantage.

I'm Rory Gibbs. I didn't pick up an oar until I was 18. I finished my international career as an Olympic champion. Here's what happened in between.

GoldParis 2024 Olympics, men's eight

My story

From rugby to gold

  • 18
    First time in a boatI'd played rugby and a few other sports. Rowing came late, and I had a lot to learn.
  • GB
    Eat, sleep, row, repeatBreaking into the GB team meant a huge training load. For periods I slept 12 hours a night, because that was what recovery took.
  • 2021
    Tokyo OlympicsWe fell short of our goal and very nearly crashed into another crew. A hard lesson, and the start of the rebuild.
  • 2024
    Paris OlympicsThree years later, my crew and I won gold in the men's eight.
  • Now
    Passing it onMentoring young athletes, writing, and building the Athlete Deck.

Why I do this.

For a decade I learned from Team GB psychologists, physios, nutritionists, strength coaches and teammates. Most young athletes never get near that kind of support.

The mental side of sport decides how far you go, and it can be learned. I want every young athlete, in every sport, to have the tools I wish I'd had at 15.

Talent opens the door. Character walks you through it.

I also speak in schools, from assemblies to sports scholar sessions. Ask about a talk
