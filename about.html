About me

A late start isn't a disadvantage.

I'm Rory Gibbs. I didn't pick up an oar until I was 18. I finished my international career as an Olympic champion. Here's what happened in between, and why I do what I do now.

GoldParis 2024 Olympics, men's eight
2×World Champion
5×European Champion
6×World Cup winner
My story

From rugby to gold

  1. 18

    First time in a boat

    I'd played rugby and a few other sports. Rowing came late, and I had a lot to learn.

  2. GB

    Eat, sleep, row, repeat

    Breaking into the GB team meant a huge training load. For periods I slept 12 hours a night, because that's what recovery took.

  3. 2021

    Tokyo Olympics

    We fell short of our goal and very nearly crashed into another crew. A hard lesson, and the start of the rebuild.

  4. 2024

    Paris Olympics

    Three years later, my crew and I won gold in the men's eight.

  5. Now

    Passing it on

    Content, the Athlete Deck, the book, courses, seminars and mentoring. All for young athletes and their parents.

Why I do this.

For a decade I learned from Team GB psychologists, physios, nutritionists, strength coaches and teammates. Most young athletes never get near that kind of support, and most parents are left guessing.

The mental side of sport decides how far you go, and it can be learned. I want every young athlete in every sport, and every parent behind them, to have the tools I wish I'd had.

Talent opens the door. Character walks you through it.
