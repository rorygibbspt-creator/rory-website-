Why me

Every sport runs on the same fundamentals.

Most young athletes have a training plan. Very few have a system for the mental side of sport, and that's the side that decides how far they go.

I help developing athletes handle the things nobody teaches them: selection, relationships with coaches, pressure, injuries, goal setting and bouncing back. From around 14 onwards these skills start to matter, and the athletes who build them early pull away.

Who it's for

Three kinds of athlete.

The aspiring elite

Chasing the next level

Aiming for county, national or scholarship level and needs the mental game to match the physical one.

The talented but stuck

Not showing it on the day

Has the ability, but nerves, inconsistency or motivation keep getting in the way when it counts.

The all rounder

Wants to get better

Simply wants to improve, enjoy their sport more and build habits that carry into the rest of life.

Open to every sport and level, for athletes aged 14 to 22. Most of my athletes are rowers, but the principles carry across every sport.

Questions

Mentoring, answered.

Who is it suitable for?
Athletes of any sport and level, from those aiming to represent their country to those who simply want to improve and enjoy it more. The common thread is wanting to take the mental side seriously. I mostly work with rowers aged 14 to 21, and I often introduce athletes around 14 to 16 to strength and conditioning.
How is it delivered?
One to one, weekly, online or in person. During the holidays I can write a bespoke physical programme matched to the demands of your sport.
Why is there an application?
I deliberately work with a handful of athletes at once so each gets my full attention. That's why places are by application rather than instant sign up.
What does it cost?
It's a bespoke service, so pricing depends on what you need. We'll talk it through on a short, free discovery call, or I can email my pricing over.
