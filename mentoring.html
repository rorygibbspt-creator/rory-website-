Questions

Mentoring, answered.

Who is it for?
Athletes aged 14 to 22 in any sport, from those aiming to represent their country to those who simply want to improve and enjoy it more. Most of my athletes are rowers, but the principles carry across every sport.
Why is there an application?
I keep numbers small so every athlete gets my full attention. That's why places are by application rather than instant sign up.
What does it cost?
It's bespoke, so it depends on what you need. We'll talk it through on a short, free discovery call, or I can email my pricing over.
Can parents get in touch?
Yes. Plenty of enquiries come from parents, so feel free to fill in the form for your athlete.
